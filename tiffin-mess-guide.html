STUDENT FOOD GUIDE

How to choose a tiffin or mess service.

Compare meal plans, timing, delivery coverage and the details that matter to a student’s daily routine.

CHECK THESE FIRST

Key details

🍱 Plan

Check whether daily, weekly or monthly plans are offered and what each plan includes.

🥗 Meal type

Confirm vegetarian or other available meal choices, meal count and serving expectations.

🕒 Timing

Check breakfast, lunch and dinner timing and whether delivery windows fit your schedule.

🛵 Delivery

Confirm whether delivery is available at your hostel, PG, college or study location.

💳 Price

Ask about plan price, deposit, delivery charges, extras and cancellation or pause rules.

📞 Contact

Confirm the current menu, service area and availability directly before subscribing.

QUESTIONS

Ask before subscribing

How many meals are included?Confirm the exact meal count and serving schedule.
What is the delivery area?Make sure your exact location is covered rather than relying only on a broad area name.
Can the plan be paused?Ask how absences, holidays and plan changes are handled.
What is the current menu?Confirm current food details because menus can change.
