3. Public Directory Sourcing & Third-Party Disclaimer

INDEPENDENT INFORMATIONAL STATUS:

StudentHub is an independent public information and discovery directory and is not the owner, operator, agent, broker, landlord, mess operator, or library operator of the third-party businesses listed on the Platform.

Sources of Information: Listing information may be compiled from:

  • Publicly available information, street signboards, and public advertisements.
  • Business websites, public maps, and local directories.
  • Information submitted directly by business owners or their authorized representatives.

Note: Publicly available information does not automatically mean that every listing has been independently verified. Users should refer to our verification indicators.

4. Verification Classification System

StudentHub utilizes three distinct listing indicators for clarity and transparency:

🟢 Verified

Information confirmed directly with the owner or authorized representative.

🔵 Public Listing

Information collected from public sources; pending formal owner confirmation.

🟡 Information Update Required

Listing undergoing periodic review or requiring updated vacancy details.

5. Business Owner Update & Takedown Policy

Business owners and authorized representatives have full rights to manage their listing representation on StudentHub:

  • Update Request: Correct property name, phone numbers, WhatsApp, address, rent, vacancies, amenities, photos, menus, or library timings.
  • Removal Request: Request complete removal and delisting of the property from the directory.
Takedown & Correction Procedure:

Request Received → Authorization Check → Information Verification → Listing Updated / Removed → Confirmation Sent

We aim to process valid requests within 24 to 48 hours upon receiving communication via WhatsApp/Phone at +91 99297 18264 or email at satpalswami22742@gmail.com.

6. Report Listing & Anti-Fraud Policy

Users may report inaccurate, misleading, or fraudulent listings (e.g., incorrect phone, closed business, misleading rent, fake photos, fraud/scams, or owner disputes).

Reported items undergo administrative review and verification, leading to appropriate updates, warnings, or immediate removal from the platform.

7. Student Safety & Financial Caution Disclaimer

⚠️ CRITICAL FINANCIAL ADVISORY:

Do NOT transfer money to anyone solely because a business is listed on StudentHub.

StudentHub does not collect hostel deposits, rent, tiffin payments, or library fees unless a specific official payment feature is introduced and clearly identified on the Platform.

  • Hostel/PG: Students and parents are strongly advised to perform a physical inspection before committing to rent.
  • Tiffin/Mess: Independently verify food quality, hygiene, pricing, and delivery reliability.
  • Library: Independently verify seat availability, fees, operating timings, and AC/power backup facilities.

8. Third-Party Disclaimer & Zero Brokerage

StudentHub is not an owner, broker, agent, guarantor, or payment intermediary for third-party businesses. A listing on StudentHub does not constitute an endorsement, recommendation, certification, partnership, sponsorship, or guarantee.

StudentHub currently does not charge students or parents brokerage or booking commissions for ordinary directory access. If paid promotions or business advertising are introduced in the future, paid promotion shall not constitute an endorsement.

9. Data Sharing & Strict Zero-Sale Policy

StudentHub does NOT sell personal data (name, email, mobile number) to third-party telemarketers or data brokers.

Personal data is only processed under necessary legal and operational circumstances:

  • With the user's explicit consent when reaching out to listings.
  • Through essential technical hosting and authentication infrastructure.
  • When required to comply with applicable legal obligations, court orders, or law enforcement.
  • For security and fraud prevention.

Direct Communication Caveat: When you choose to call, WhatsApp, or contact a listed business, your interaction occurs directly with that third party and is subject to their private practices.

10. Account Deletion & Data Retention Schedule

Users may request account deletion through email, WhatsApp, or available dashboard controls. Account deletion will remove or anonymize applicable personal data from active systems, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and backup-retention requirements.

  • Account Data: Retained for the duration of active account status.
  • Listing Data: Retained as long as the listing remains operational or until an owner removal request is processed.
  • Security Logs: Retained for a limited period strictly for security analysis.
  • Technical Backups: Backup copies may be retained for a limited period according to our backup, security, and disaster-recovery procedures and may persist temporarily after deletion from active systems.
