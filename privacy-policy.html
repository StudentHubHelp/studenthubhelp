1. Overview

StudentHubHelp helps students, parents and other visitors discover student-oriented services and places such as hostels, PG accommodations, tiffin or food services, libraries, cafes, bookstores and related local services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit or use the website, interact with listings, submit information, contact the platform, create an account or otherwise use available features.

It also explains an important distinction between personal information and directory information.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use StudentHubHelp, information handled by the platform may include:

  • Account information such as name, email address and other information voluntarily provided during account-related activities.
  • Information submitted through listing, correction, claim, report or contact forms.
  • Property or business information provided by an owner, representative or other contributor.
  • Technical information reasonably required to operate, secure and improve the website.
  • Information contained in communications sent to StudentHubHelp.

StudentHubHelp does not need every visitor to create an account merely to browse publicly available directory information.

Purpose limitation

Information should be handled for legitimate and relevant purposes connected with operating the platform, providing requested functionality, maintaining security, responding to requests, managing listings and meeting applicable legal obligations.

4. Verification & Review Status

StudentHubHelp may use different listing states to communicate the current review status of directory information.

🟢 Verified

The applicable StudentHubHelp review or confirmation process for the listing has been completed based on the information available to the platform at that time.

🟡 Pending Review

The listing is awaiting review or additional confirmation. Users should treat important information as unconfirmed until the applicable review is completed.

🔵 Public / Directory Information

Information may be displayed because it is useful for discovery. Publication alone does not mean the information has been independently verified.

What "Verified" does not mean

A Verified label does not constitute a permanent guarantee of safety, quality, ownership, legality, availability, price, service quality, future accuracy or suitability.

Verification should be understood according to its scope, method and date. Circumstances can change after a review.

5. Unverified / Pending Review Listings

Some listings may be published before StudentHubHelp completes its internal review.

Unverified or Pending Review means that the relevant review has not yet been completed. It does not automatically mean that the listing is false, unsafe, fraudulent or illegal.

Before relying on an unverified listing

Students and parents should independently confirm important information directly with the relevant property or service.

  • Exact address and location
  • Current phone number or contact person
  • Current rent, price and additional charges
  • Availability
  • Facilities and amenities
  • Rules and restrictions
  • Deposit, refund and payment terms

6. Accuracy, Freshness & Corrections

Directory information can become outdated. Prices, availability, phone numbers, addresses, facilities, ownership, operating status and other details may change without immediate notice.

StudentHubHelp may review listings individually, process owner-submitted updates, consider credible user reports and make reasonable corrections when reliable information becomes available.

🕒 Review & freshness

A listing may be reviewed again when material information changes, a credible correction is received, a report is submitted or StudentHubHelp determines that another review is appropriate.

✏️ Corrections

If you identify incorrect, outdated, duplicate or misleading information, please use an available reporting or contact channel so the information can be assessed.

A correction request does not automatically establish that the information is incorrect. StudentHubHelp may request reasonable supporting information before changing or removing a listing.

7. Owner Claims & Requests

A property or business owner or authorized representative may contact StudentHubHelp regarding a listing.

Depending on the request, available options may include:

  • Claiming or requesting recognition of a listing.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Providing updated business or property details.
  • Reporting duplicate, misleading or unauthorized information.
  • Requesting removal where appropriate and legally permissible.

StudentHubHelp may request reasonable evidence of ownership or authority before making changes, particularly where a request could affect another person's information or a public listing.

8. Reports & Fraud

Users are encouraged to report suspicious, misleading, fraudulent, duplicated or materially incorrect directory information.

Reports may be reviewed using information reasonably available to StudentHubHelp. A report does not automatically prove that an allegation is true.

Where appropriate, StudentHubHelp may update a listing, temporarily restrict its presentation, request additional information or remove information that does not meet applicable platform or legal requirements.

Users should also contact appropriate authorities or financial institutions directly when they believe they have experienced fraud, theft, impersonation or another serious offence.

9. Student Safety

Safety reminder

Do not treat a StudentHubHelp listing or verification label as a substitute for your own due diligence.

Before visiting or paying for a property or service, users should consider independently confirming:

  • The actual location and physical condition.
  • Identity and authority of the person dealing with them.
  • Current price, deposit and payment requirements.
  • Written terms, rules and refund conditions.
  • Whether the service is suitable for their individual needs.

Never share OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, card PINs or other sensitive authentication information with an unknown person claiming to represent a listing.

10. Payments & Brokerage

StudentHubHelp is primarily a discovery and directory platform. Unless a specific feature expressly states otherwise, StudentHubHelp is not the seller, landlord, property manager, broker or service provider represented by a listing.

Users may communicate or transact directly with third parties. Any rent, deposit, booking fee, food payment, service payment, brokerage, refund or other financial arrangement should be independently confirmed with the relevant party.

StudentHubHelp does not guarantee the outcome of a transaction between a user and a third-party listing.

11. Data Sharing

StudentHubHelp may use service providers and technical infrastructure necessary to operate the website and its features.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide or maintain requested services.
  • Protect platform security and prevent abuse.
  • Investigate reports or suspected fraud.
  • Respond to lawful legal or governmental requirements.
  • Protect rights, safety and property where legally permitted.

StudentHubHelp does not intend to sell personal data merely because users browse the directory. Any data handling remains subject to the purposes, legal bases and requirements applicable to the particular processing activity.

12. Data Retention

Personal information and other records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, legitimate operational requirements, security, dispute handling, fraud prevention, legal obligations or other applicable purposes.

When information is no longer reasonably required and there is no applicable reason to retain it, appropriate deletion, anonymisation or other handling may be considered.

Retention periods can differ depending on the type of information and the circumstances in which it was collected.

13. Privacy Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights and choices concerning their digital personal data.

Depending on the applicable legal framework and processing circumstances, these may include:

  • Requesting information about applicable processing.
  • Requesting correction or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • Requesting erasure where legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is legally available.
  • Raising privacy-related grievances through an available grievance channel.

A request may require reasonable verification of identity or authority, particularly where the request could affect another person's information.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 have a phased commencement framework. StudentHubHelp will apply mandatory provisions to the extent and from the dates they become applicable to the platform.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or unlawfully restrict a right that cannot legally be excluded.

14. Cookies & Storage

StudentHubHelp may use browser storage, cookies or similar technical mechanisms where required for website functionality, preferences, authentication, security, analytics or other legitimate purposes.

Browser settings may allow users to restrict or remove certain cookies or stored information. Some functionality may not work correctly if essential storage mechanisms are disabled.

Third-party services integrated into the website may have their own privacy and cookie practices.

15. Students & Families

StudentHubHelp is designed to help students and families discover local services and accommodations.

Parents and guardians should supervise minors' interactions with third-party properties and service providers where appropriate.

Users should not submit another person's personal information unless they have an appropriate lawful basis or authority to do so.

If you believe personal information has been submitted or published improperly, please use the available privacy or grievance channel so the issue can be reviewed.

16. Security & Breach Response

StudentHubHelp uses reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved to help protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or loss.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore use strong passwords where applicable, protect their authentication information and avoid sharing sensitive credentials.

If StudentHubHelp becomes aware of a relevant security or data incident, it may take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, remediate and provide notifications or disclosures where required by applicable law.

17. User Submissions & Intellectual Property

If you submit listing information, corrections, reviews, reports, images, text or other material, you should have the necessary rights or authority to submit that material.

By submitting information for platform use, you permit StudentHubHelp to use, store, reproduce, format, display and process the submitted material as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant feature, subject to applicable law.

StudentHubHelp may remove or restrict submitted content where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, safety, accuracy, abuse prevention, privacy or platform integrity.

Nothing in this policy transfers ownership of intellectual property that a user or third party lawfully owns.

18. Applicable Law

StudentHubHelp intends to operate in accordance with applicable Indian laws and regulations relevant to its activities.

Depending on the nature of the processing or service involved, applicable requirements may include data-protection, consumer, information-technology, contractual, intellectual-property and other relevant laws.

Legal requirements may change over time. This policy may therefore be updated when platform practices, applicable law or regulatory requirements materially change.

Where a mandatory legal requirement conflicts with a statement in this policy, the mandatory legal requirement will prevail to the extent required.

19. Privacy & Grievance Contact

For privacy concerns, personal-data requests, listing disputes, correction requests, removal requests, safety reports or other platform grievances, users should use the official contact or reporting channel made available by StudentHubHelp.

To help us process a request efficiently, please provide enough information to identify the relevant listing, account or issue. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment-card PINs or other unnecessary sensitive credentials.
