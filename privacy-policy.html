1. Platform Identity & Purpose

StudentHubHelp ("StudentHubHelp", "StudentHub", "Platform", "we", "our" or "us") operates an independent student discovery and information platform.

The platform helps students and parents discover useful services such as:

  • Hostels and PG accommodations
  • Tiffin and Mess services
  • Libraries and study spaces
  • Cafes
  • Book Stores and study-related businesses

StudentHubHelp is designed to help users discover information and contact listed businesses directly. The platform is not automatically a landlord, hostel operator, food provider, library operator, cafe operator, book seller, broker or agent for third-party listings.

2. Information We Collect & Why

Depending on how you use StudentHubHelp, we may process information required to provide account, discovery, review, booking and administrative functionality.

Information Examples Purpose
Account Information Name, email, mobile number and account role Authentication, account management and dashboard functionality.
Student Activity Reviews, bookings and selected service information Providing requested platform features and displaying relevant records to the authenticated user.
Owner Information Business name, address, phone, facilities and listing information Creating and maintaining public directory listings.
Technical Information Browser, device, session and technical data Security, troubleshooting, performance and essential functionality.

We do not intend to collect personal information merely because it is technically possible to do so. Information should have a functional, security, operational or legal purpose.

3. Public Directory Information

StudentHubHelp may contain information about third-party hostels, PGs, tiffin services, libraries, cafes and book stores.

Listing information may come from:

  • Information publicly available on the internet.
  • Public maps, directories and business sources.
  • Public advertisements or signage.
  • Information submitted by owners or authorized representatives.
  • Corrections and updates received through the platform.

Public availability of information does not automatically mean that every detail has been independently verified by StudentHubHelp.

4. Listing Verification Classification

To improve transparency, listings may use different information indicators.

🟢 Verified

Information has been confirmed directly with the owner or an authorized representative, subject to the scope of the confirmation.

🔵 Public Listing

Information is available from public sources and may not yet have been formally confirmed by the owner.

🟡 Information Update Required

Some information may require review, correction or a fresh update.

5. Business Owner Updates, Claims & Removal

Owners and authorized representatives may contact StudentHubHelp to request correction, ownership claim, update or removal of a listing.

Examples of information that may be updated

  • Business or property name
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Address and location information
  • Facilities and amenities
  • Vacancy or availability information
  • Pricing or other listing information
  • Photos and service details
  • Timing and operational information

General correction process

Request Received → Authorization Check → Information Review → Update / Removal → Confirmation

Requests may require reasonable evidence of authorization before ownership or sensitive listing information is changed.

6. Report Listing & Anti-Fraud Policy

Students, parents, owners and other users may report information that appears inaccurate, misleading, outdated or potentially fraudulent.

Examples include:

  • Incorrect phone number
  • Closed or permanently unavailable business
  • Misleading property information
  • Incorrect pricing or vacancy information
  • Potentially misleading photographs
  • Ownership disputes
  • Suspected fraud or scam-related information

Reports may be reviewed by the platform administration and may result in correction, warning, temporary restriction or removal where appropriate.

7. Student & Parent Safety Disclaimer

⚠️ Important Financial & Safety Advisory

Do not transfer money, share sensitive information or make a major financial commitment solely because a business or property appears on StudentHubHelp.

Students and parents should independently verify important information before making payments or entering into agreements.

Hostel / PG

Where possible, students and parents should inspect the property, confirm rent and deposit requirements, understand applicable rules and independently verify availability before making a commitment.

Tiffin / Mess

Users should independently verify food quality, hygiene, pricing, menu, delivery arrangements and service reliability.

Library

Users should confirm seat availability, fees, operating hours, facilities, access rules and other important conditions directly with the library.

Cafe & Book Store

Users should independently confirm pricing, availability, operating hours and other service information before visiting or purchasing.

8. Payments, Brokerage & Third-Party Transactions

StudentHubHelp is an information and discovery platform. Ordinary directory access does not constitute a brokerage relationship between StudentHubHelp and a listed business.

Unless a clearly identified official payment functionality is introduced, StudentHubHelp does not collect hostel deposits, rent, tiffin payments, library fees or other third-party service payments on behalf of listed businesses.

When a student or parent contacts a listed business directly, the resulting transaction or agreement is between the user and that third party.

9. Data Sharing & Personal Data Protection

StudentHubHelp does not sell personal account information such as names, email addresses or mobile numbers to data brokers or telemarketing companies.

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary for:

  • Authentication and essential platform functionality.
  • Hosting, database and technical infrastructure.
  • Security, fraud prevention and abuse investigation.
  • User-requested communication with third-party businesses.
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations, lawful requests or court orders.

When you choose to contact a listed business by phone, WhatsApp or another external communication method, that interaction is directly with the third party and may be governed by its own privacy practices.

10. Account Deletion & Data Retention

Users may request deletion of their account or applicable personal information through available platform controls or by contacting StudentHubHelp.

Certain information may need to remain temporarily available where reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal compliance or technical backup purposes.

General retention principles

  • Account information may be retained while an account remains active.
  • Listing information may remain while a listing is operational or until a valid removal request is processed.
  • Security and technical records may be retained for a limited period for operational and security purposes.
  • Backup copies may persist temporarily after deletion from active systems.

11. Cookies, Local Storage & Advertising

StudentHubHelp may use essential browser storage, session mechanisms and preference storage to support authentication and website functionality.

Search preferences or user interface settings may also be stored locally where required for a better experience.

StudentHubHelp may introduce advertising services in the future. If third-party advertising is used, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable settings.

12. Students, Minors & Family Safety

StudentHubHelp is intended for students and families. Where a student is under 18, parents or lawful guardians should participate in important decisions relating to accommodation, payments and third-party services.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information through public reviews, comments or listing-related communications.

13. Technical & Organisational Security

StudentHubHelp uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Depending on the feature, safeguards may include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • Authentication controls
  • Role-based access controls
  • Database security policies
  • Restricted administrative access
  • Security and fraud monitoring

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also maintain strong, private passwords and avoid sharing login credentials.
