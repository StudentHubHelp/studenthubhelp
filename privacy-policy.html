1. Platform Identity & Operational Mission

StudentHub Shekhawati ("StudentHub", "Platform", "we", "our", or "us"), founded and directed by Satpal Swami, operates as a free, independent, public informational discovery network at studenthubhelp.github.io/studenthubhelp/.

Our objective is to facilitate seamless, zero-brokerage access to verified student accommodations (Hostels/PGs), hygienic daily tiffin services, and 24/7 self-study libraries for students preparing for competitive examinations (NEET, IIT-JEE, NDA, RRB, REET) across Sikar, Neem Ka Thana, and the Shekhawati region of Rajasthan.

2. Public Directory Data Sourcing & Non-Affiliation Notice

IMPORTANT NOTICE TO BUSINESS OWNERS & GENERAL PUBLIC:

All business listings, hostel names, tiffin center contact details, library addresses, and phone numbers published on StudentHub are compiled strictly from publicly accessible records, street signboards, public advertisements, and local directory listings for non-commercial student welfare.

  • No Commercial Partnership / Endorsement: The publication of any business name or contact number on this website does not imply any personal contract, commercial partnership, formal meeting, endorsement, or financial sponsorship between StudentHub and the respective property or mess owner.
  • Zero Brokerage Guarantee: StudentHub is strictly a search directory and does not charge any brokerage fees, commissions, or booking deposits from students, parents, or property owners.
  • Intermediary Safe Harbor: StudentHub operates under the safe harbor protections of Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 as an intermediary providing access to public knowledge.

4. Student Due Diligence & Safety Advisory

StudentHub facilitates direct peer-to-peer communication between students and property managers. We strongly advise students and parents to adhere to the following safety practices:

  • Physical Inspection: Always visit the hostel, inspect room hygiene, water quality, and security measures in person before paying any token or security deposit.
  • Direct Negotiation: Inquire about written rental agreements, refund policies, and electricity bill terms directly with the warden or owner.
  • Financial Non-Liability: StudentHub does not collect rent or deposits and is not liable for private tenancy disputes, food quality discrepancies, or personal belongings.

5. User Data Rights Under DPDPA 2023 (India)

When you create a user account on StudentHub, we collect minimal information (Name, Email, Mobile Number) strictly to authenticate your dashboard. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:
