3. Public Directory Sourcing & Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

INDEPENDENT INFORMATIONAL STATUS:

StudentHub is an independent public information and discovery directory. StudentHub is NOT the owner, operator, landlord, warden, mess cook, or library manager of the third-party businesses listed on the platform.

Sources of Directory Data: Listings on this platform are compiled from:

  • Publicly available street signboards, banners, and brochures in Sikar coaching zones.
  • Public online directories, Google Maps, and public business advertisements.
  • Information voluntarily submitted by property owners or authorized wardens.

Crucial Distinction: Public availability of information does NOT automatically imply that every single listing has been physically verified by our team. Users must rely on the verification indicators described below.

4. Three-Tier Listing Verification Framework

To ensure maximum transparency, StudentHub categorizes listings into three distinct operational states:

🟢 Verified Partner

Information confirmed directly by the property owner, warden, or authorized manager.

🔵 Public Listing

Information sourced from public boards/records; awaiting owner-portal registration.

🟡 Update Required

Information flagged for review or older vacancy data undergoing re-verification.

5. Business Owner Rights: Live Update & Instant Takedown Policy

Property owners, mess operators, and library managers retain complete control over how their business appears. If you are the authorized representative of any listed business, you have the right to:

  • Update Details: Correct your phone numbers, WhatsApp, room vacancies, pricing, meal menus, or amenities free of charge.
  • Request Removal / Delisting: Request immediate and permanent removal of your business listing from StudentHub.
Structured Takedown Process:

Request Received → Authorization Check → Data Verification → Listing Removed/Updated → Confirmation Sent

We aim to process all verified removal and correction requests within 24 to 48 business hours upon receiving communication via WhatsApp/Phone at +91 99297 18264 or email at satpalswami22742@gmail.com.

6. Report Incorrect Listing & Fraud Prevention

Students, parents, and community members can flag any discrepancy (wrong phone number, closed business, misleading rents, fake photos, or fraud attempts) directly to Director Satpal Swami.

Upon receiving a report, StudentHub initiates an administrative review. Listings proven fraudulent or permanently closed are purged immediately from the live index.

7. Student Due Diligence & Financial Safety Advisory

⚠️ CRITICAL PAYMENT SAFETY WARNING:

Do NOT transfer money, advance deposits, or room booking tokens online to anyone solely because a business is listed on StudentHub.

StudentHub NEVER collects hostel security deposits, room rent, tiffin subscriptions, or library fees. Any financial transaction is strictly a direct matter between the student and the property owner.

  • Hostel/PG Safety: Always conduct a physical room inspection, check water/power backup, CCTV security, and demand a written rent receipt from the warden.
  • Tiffin/Mess Safety: Verify meal hygiene, tasting sample, and monthly delivery terms before long-term subscription.
  • Library Safety: Confirm dedicated AC operational hours, reserved seat assignment, and quiet cabin rules prior to payment.

8. Zero Brokerage & No Hidden Commission Policy

StudentHub provides 100% free directory access to students and parents. We do not act as real estate brokers, middlemen, or commission agents. If any third party demands a broker commission under the name of StudentHub, report them to us immediately.

Future Advertising Disclosure: If sponsored or featured business placements are introduced in the future, paid promotional status will be clearly labeled and shall not constitute an endorsement or guarantee by StudentHub.

9. Strict Zero-Sale Data Policy & Technical Sharing

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade student, parent, or owner personal information to third-party telemarketers, coaching institutes, or data brokers.

Personal data is only processed under the following strict boundaries:

  • User Consent: Direct phone/WhatsApp communication initiated by the student with a listed property.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure database and authentication hosting (Supabase infrastructure).
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly mandated by lawful government authorities or Indian law enforcement.

10. Account Deletion & Data Retention Schedule

Users can request permanent account deletion via their dashboard, email, or WhatsApp. Upon receiving a valid request, account credentials and profile records are deleted from active databases.

  • Active Accounts: Retained for the duration of active platform usage.
  • Directory Listings: Retained as long as the listing remains operational or until an owner takedown request is executed.
  • Technical Backups: Flushed in accordance with standard cryptographic backup rotation cycles.

11. Cookies, LocalStorage & Google AdSense Policy

StudentHub uses browser LocalStorage solely to maintain user authentication and search filter preferences. To sustain free student services, StudentHub may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and affiliated third-party advertising vendors.

Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on user visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

12. Minors & Teenage Student Safety

A substantial number of competitive exam aspirants in Sikar are minors (under 18) preparing for NEET/JEE. StudentHub does not knowingly collect sensitive biometric or financial data from minors. We strongly advise parents and guardians to participate in all housing decisions and room visits.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. These measures include HTTPS SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption, strict database row-level security (RLS), and restricted administrator credentials.
