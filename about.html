Who We Are

What is StudentHubHelp?

A student-first platform designed around the places and services students need in their everyday academic life.

StudentHubHelp is an India-focused student discovery platform that brings useful local information into one searchable experience.

Students moving to a new city or preparing for college often need to search for several things at the same time — a place to stay, affordable food, a library or study space, a cafe, books and other everyday services.

Instead of making students search through scattered sources, StudentHubHelp is being built around a simple idea: make relevant student-focused places easier to discover, understand and connect with.

The platform combines category-based discovery, location-aware search and listing information so students can explore options based on their city, area and individual needs.

Our Purpose

Why we built StudentHubHelp

Finding student essentials should not feel like solving a different search problem every time.

The student problem

When students move to a new city, useful local information can be spread across search results, social groups, recommendations and individual businesses. Comparing options can become time-consuming and confusing.

The StudentHubHelp approach

We are building a focused discovery experience where accommodation, food, study spaces, cafes, bookstores and other student-relevant services can be explored through categories, locations and searchable listing information.

Mission & Vision

To make student life easier by bringing useful, student-focused accommodation, food, study and everyday local services into a simple discovery platform.

Our Vision

To build a trusted student discovery ecosystem that helps students discover relevant places and services across cities and student communities throughout India.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A few common questions about StudentHubHelp and the platform.

What is StudentHubHelp?

StudentHubHelp is a student-focused discovery platform designed to help students discover hostels, PGs, tiffin and mess services, libraries, cafes, bookstores and other useful local services across cities in India.

What can students find on StudentHubHelp?

Students can explore categories such as hostels and PGs, tiffin and mess services, libraries, cafes and bookstores. Depending on the listing, information may include location, contact details, pricing, facilities and other relevant details.

Is StudentHubHelp available across India?

StudentHubHelp is designed as an India-focused platform and is being expanded across cities and student communities. The actual availability of listings varies by city and category.

Can property owners and service providers join?

StudentHubHelp is designed to connect students with relevant local accommodation and student-focused services. Owners and service providers can use the available listing process to provide information about their property or service.

Can listing information change?

Yes. Information such as pricing, availability, facilities, contact details and operating information can change. Students should confirm important details directly with the relevant property or service provider.
