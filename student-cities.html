ABOUT STUDENTHUBHELP

One place to explore student-life essentials

StudentHubHelp is a student-focused discovery platform that organises local accommodation and everyday student-service information into searchable city and category guides. Students can review available listing details, locations and facilities, then contact providers to confirm current terms.

City coverage grows as local listings and area information are prepared. A listing or position in this directory is not a ranking, endorsement or guarantee. Always verify rent, deposit, food, timings, safety arrangements and availability directly with the provider.

QUICK ANSWERS

Student city search FAQs

Which cities are currently covered by StudentHubHelp?

StudentHubHelp currently publishes 12 city guide pages: Sikar, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kota, Delhi, Patna, Jhunjhunu, Pune, Gurugram, Coimbatore, Jaipur and Lucknow. Lucknow currently has no active listing records in the connected directory data.

What is the difference between Sikar and Kota for students?

Sikar currently has platform listings across hostels and PGs, tiffin, libraries, cafes and bookstores. Kota has hostel and PG plus tiffin listings; its 15 library references are from an external directory, not StudentHubHelp. Compare exact locality, commute, facilities and provider terms before choosing.

How should I choose a hostel or PG near coaching?

Check the exact address and commute, total rent and deposit, food arrangements, room sharing, Wi-Fi and power backup, CCTV or other safety arrangements, visitor rules and written terms. Visit where possible and confirm availability directly before paying.

How do I find a library, tiffin service or study cafe?

Open the relevant city guide and check categories with active StudentHubHelp listings. Kota and Jaipur also show separately labelled external library or tiffin references; these are not platform listings, so verify providers independently. Not every service category is available in every city.

How can I request a new city or add my hostel or PG?

Use the Contact Us page to request a student city guide. Hostel, PG, library, tiffin, cafe and bookstore providers can use the Add Listing page to submit their details for review.
