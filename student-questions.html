COMMON QUESTIONS

Accommodation

How do I choose between a hostel and PG?Compare the total cost, sharing, food, rules, facilities, location and the routine that suits you.
What should I check before paying?Confirm current availability, price, deposit terms, rules, address and what is included.
Should I choose only by distance?No. Distance matters, but total cost, safety, facilities and daily travel conditions matter too.
FOOD & STUDY

Daily student services

🍱 How do I compare tiffin plans?

Check meals included, timing, food type, delivery coverage, price and pause/cancellation terms.

📚 What makes a library useful?

Check timing, fee, seating, location, study environment, connectivity and facilities.

☕ Can a cafe be used for studying?

It depends on the cafe. Check seating, hours, connectivity and whether longer study stays are suitable.

📖 How do I find a bookstore?

Check location and whether the store offers the books, stationery or print/copy services you need.
