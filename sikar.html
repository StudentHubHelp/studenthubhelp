Student checklist before choosing

Use this checklist before booking a room, ordering a meal plan or purchasing a library pass.

  • Confirm the exact address and map route to your coaching centre or college.
  • Ask for the complete price, deposit, included services and refund terms in writing.
  • For hostels, inspect room sharing, CCTV, visitor rules, water, power backup and food arrangements.
  • For tiffin, confirm the menu, meal timings, delivery coverage and trial options.
  • For libraries, check seat availability, opening hours, noise rules, charging points and access terms.
  • Do not pay a deposit before verifying the property and provider.
