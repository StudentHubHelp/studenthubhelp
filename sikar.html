BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Student checklist for Sikar

Use this checklist before booking accommodation, subscribing to a meal plan or buying a library pass.

  • Confirm the exact address and route to your coaching centre, college or workplace.
  • Ask for the complete rent or fee, deposit, included services and refund terms in writing.
  • For hostels and PGs, inspect room sharing, CCTV, visitor rules, water, power backup and food arrangements.
  • For tiffin, confirm menu, meals per day, delivery area, timings and plan duration.
  • For libraries, check seat availability, access hours, shift rules, charging points and Wi-Fi.
  • Do not pay a deposit before verifying the property, provider and written terms.
