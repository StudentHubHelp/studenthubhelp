Monthly vs daily tiffin: what should you compare?

The useful choice depends on your routine, flexibility needs, service terms and total cost.

FactorDailyMonthly
FlexibilityCan suit short or changing schedules.Can suit a regular routine, subject to provider terms.
RoutineUseful when meals vary by day.Useful when you expect regular service.
CostCompare per-meal or daily charges.Compare total monthly price and included meals.
DeliveryConfirm each service window.Confirm regular delivery coverage and timing.
TermsAsk about minimum orders and cancellation.Ask about pause, absence and refund terms.
Actual plans differ by provider. Confirm the current price, menu, timing and delivery area directly.
