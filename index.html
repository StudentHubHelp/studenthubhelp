Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about StudentHubHelp:

Q1. What is StudentHubHelp?

StudentHubHelp is Sikar's premier free student discovery network connecting competitive exam aspirants (NEET, IIT-JEE, NDA, RRB) with 100 verified Hostels, hygienic Tiffin providers, and 24/7 AC Libraries without middlemen.

Q2. Does StudentHubHelp charge any brokerage or booking fees?

No, absolutely zero brokerage! StudentHubHelp provides 100% free directory access. You contact property owners and wardens directly via phone or WhatsApp with no commission fees.

Q3. How are hostels and PGs verified on this platform?

We verify phone numbers, exact addresses, room availability, and amenities. We strongly recommend students and parents to conduct an in-person room inspection before paying deposits.

Q4. Are there separate listings for Boys and Girls hostels?

Yes! Our directory features 50 dedicated Boys Hostels and 50 dedicated Girls Hostels with CCTV surveillance and resident warden details in Sikar.

Q5. How can I order or subscribe to a daily tiffin service?

Visit the Tiffin & Mess page, browse daily Lunch and Dinner thali menus, and directly tap "Call Now" or "WhatsApp" to order a trial meal delivered to your hostel or room.

Q6. How do I reserve a seat in a 24/7 AC study library?

Navigate to the Libraries page, filter by coaching zone, and select your preferred shift (Morning: 6 AM - 2 PM, Evening: 2 PM - 10 PM, or 24/7 Full Day) to reserve a cabin seat with the library manager.

Q7. Can property or mess owners list their business for free?

Yes! Click "List Property" on the navbar, fill in your property details, room capacity, and photos. Verified listings go live with zero listing fees.

Q8. What should I do if I find incorrect or closed listing information?

You can report any inaccurate or permanently closed property by clicking the report icon on any listing card or by messaging Director Satpal Swami at +91 99297 18264.

Q9. Are parent and student contact details kept private?

Yes! We adhere to India's DPDPA 2023 data protection standards. We never sell, rent, or trade student phone numbers to third-party telemarketers.

Q10. How do I contact Director Satpal Swami for student support?

You can reach Director Satpal Swami via direct call or WhatsApp at +91 99297 18264 or by email at satpalswami22742@gmail.com.
