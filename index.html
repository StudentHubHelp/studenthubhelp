GOOD TO KNOW

Helpful information for students, parents and property owners.

Contact support ↗
How do I find a hostel or PG?

Choose Hostels & PGs or use the search bar. Search by city, area, coaching landmark, budget and other details available in the listing.

Can I search for tiffin, libraries and cafes?

Yes. StudentHubHelp includes separate categories for tiffin and mess services, libraries, cafes and bookstores.

Are all listings verified?

No. A listing is marked verified only when its stored verification status is true. Review details and confirm them directly before making a decision.

How can I add my property or service?

Use the Add Listing option and submit your details through the existing owner submission process.

What should I do if I face a technical issue?

Visit Contact Us and share the page URL, a short description of the issue and a screenshot if possible.
