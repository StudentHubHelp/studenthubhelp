Contact FAQ

How do I claim a property or business listing?

Choose “Claim Listing” and provide the listing name, relevant page and reasonable non-sensitive information that helps establish your connection.

Can I correct a phone number or address?

Yes. Send the listing name, page URL if available and the correction you believe should be reviewed.

Can I request removal of a listing?

Yes. Select “Remove Listing” and explain the request. StudentHubHelp will review it according to the circumstances and applicable requirements.

Can students report outdated information?

Yes. Reports about phone numbers, addresses, facilities, images or other listing information are useful for review.

Can I contact StudentHubHelp about a hostel or PG?

Yes. You can ask about a listing or report information that needs review. Important property details should still be confirmed directly with the provider.

Can I contact you about tiffin, library, cafe or bookstore listings?

Yes. The same correction, claim and general support routes can be used for student-service listings.

What should I include in a correction request?

Include the listing name, city or area, relevant URL and a short explanation of what appears incorrect.

What should I not send?

Do not send passwords, OTPs, PINs, payment-card information or unnecessary confidential documents.

Does contacting StudentHubHelp automatically change a listing?

No. Requests are reviewed first. A request does not guarantee a particular correction, claim approval or removal outcome.

Can owners update their own listing?

Owners or authorised representatives can contact StudentHubHelp to request a claim or update review.

Can I give general feedback about the website?

Yes. Select “Feedback” and tell us what worked, what was confusing or what could make the student discovery experience better.

Where can I read the website terms?

See the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy for the platform's rules and information practices.
