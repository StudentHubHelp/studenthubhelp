Frequently asked

Common questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Is StudentHubHelp student support free?

Contacting us to ask a question or report listing information does not itself create a paid service. Any specific arrangements will be explained clearly if relevant.

Do you provide station pickup or drop services?

Student support may include guidance and journey planning. Transport availability is not guaranteed; please confirm any arrangements directly before travelling.

How can I get help in a new city?

Visit our Student Support page for available guidance and useful information for students.

Is special support available for girl students?

We provide relevant guidance and safety information where available. This is not an emergency response service; contact local emergency services if there is immediate danger.

How do I claim a property or business listing?

Select “Claim Listing” in the form and include the listing name, URL and relevant details. Claims are reviewed and are not automatically approved.

Can I correct a phone number or address?

Yes. Share the listing name, page URL if available and a clear explanation of the correction that should be reviewed.

Can I request removal of a listing?

Yes. Select “Remove Listing” and explain the request. Each request is reviewed according to the circumstances and applicable requirements.

What should I include in a correction request?

Include the listing name, city or area, relevant URL and the detail you believe is incorrect. Do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive financial information.

Does contacting StudentHubHelp automatically change a listing?

No. Requests are reviewed first. A request does not guarantee a particular correction, claim approval or removal outcome.

Where can I read the website terms?

Read the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
