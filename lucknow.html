Student checklist before choosing a provider

  • Ask for the complete price, deposit and any extra charges in writing.
  • Check facilities and service timings in person where possible.
  • For accommodation, review safety arrangements, visitor rules and agreement terms.
  • Confirm current vacancy or service availability before making payment.
