StudentHubHelp special guide

Before choosing a Hostel / PG, check safety — not just price.

A cheap room is not a good deal if the property is unsafe. Use this checklist during a visit with a parent, guardian or trusted person whenever possible.

10-point hostel / PG safety checklist

What to confirm before paying

Keep important promises in writing. Ask clear questions and inspect the actual room/property before transferring an advance.

Identity & ownershipConfirm who you are paying and who manages the property.
Agreement & depositRead notice period, deductions and refund conditions before signing.
Current availabilityDo not rely only on old photos or a message saying “one room left”.
Emergency accessAsk who to contact at night for medical, fire or security issues.
StudentHubHelp rule: never treat a listing as proof that a property is currently safe or available. Inspect and confirm important details independently.

Safety notice

Use this page as a guide — not as a substitute for emergency services.

StudentHubHelp is a discovery platform. In an emergency, contact the relevant official emergency service. Before acting on a helpline number, legal information or service availability, use the linked official source when possible.

  • Do not share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or banking credentials with anyone.
  • Do not put yourself in danger to collect screenshots, photos or other evidence.
  • For serious medical or safety emergencies, seek immediate professional help.
Official-source principle

Helpline information is presented with its source so students can cross-check the latest details. Some services can have state-specific availability or operating hours.
