Student checklist before choosing

  • Confirm exact address and route to your coaching centre or college.
  • Ask for rent, deposit, included services and refund terms in writing.
  • Inspect room sharing, CCTV, visitor rules, water, power backup and food.
  • For tiffin, confirm menu, meals per day, delivery coverage and plan duration.
  • For libraries, check seat availability, hours, shift rules, charging points and Wi-Fi.
  • Do not pay before verifying the provider and written terms.
