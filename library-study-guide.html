STUDY SPACE GUIDE

How to choose a library or study space.

Choose a study space around your real routine: location, timing, fee, seating, facilities and the environment you need.

STUDY CHECKLIST

What matters

📍 Location

Check distance and practical travel time from your hostel, PG, college or coaching centre.

🕒 Timing

Confirm opening hours, holidays and whether the schedule suits your study routine.

💳 Fee

Check monthly or other applicable fees and what is included.

🪑 Seating

Look for seating information, availability and whether the environment matches your needs.

⚡ Facilities

Check Wi-Fi, power backup, charging, AC, lockers or other listed facilities.

🤫 Environment

Ask about noise rules, study hours and the general conditions for focused work.
