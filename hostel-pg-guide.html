STUDENT ACCOMMODATION GUIDE

How to choose a hostel or PG as a student.

Use location, cost, room setup, food, facilities and safety information together instead of choosing accommodation from price alone.

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

What to check

📍 Location

Check the actual address and travel time to your coaching centre, college, workplace, market and essential services.

💰 Total cost

Look beyond monthly rent. Ask about deposit, electricity, maintenance, food, laundry and other recurring charges.

🛏️ Room & sharing

Confirm room type, number of occupants, bed availability, storage and what is included.

🍱 Food

If food is offered, confirm meal type, timing, menu expectations and whether it is included in the stated price.

🛡️ Safety

Check entry rules, emergency arrangements, visitor policy, basic security and whether important information is clearly provided.

📞 Contact

Keep the property phone number and address and confirm current availability directly before making a commitment.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

Before paying or booking

What is included in the monthly price?Clarify food, electricity, maintenance, Wi-Fi and other recurring charges.
What is the deposit and refund process?Ask for the amount, conditions and expected refund timeline.
What are the rules?Confirm timing, visitors, cooking, noise, study hours and other property-specific rules.
Is the information current?Confirm availability, price and facilities directly because these can change.
StudentHubHelp listing information is intended for discovery. Students should verify important terms, availability and payment conditions directly with the property.
