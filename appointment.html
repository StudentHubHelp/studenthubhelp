How appointments work

When should you book?

Appointments are useful when your requirement needs a direct discussion instead of a quick website answer.

🎓 Student support

Discuss a student-related requirement, platform help, or a question that needs direct assistance.

🏠 Property & listing discussion

Discuss a property, listing correction, owner claim, or information that needs a deeper review.

🤝 Owner, business & partnership

Use an appointment request for owner support, business enquiries, collaborations or partnership discussions.

💡 Website feedback & other requests

If you have an idea, feedback or another platform-related matter that is easier to explain in a conversation, you can request an appointment.

Appointment FAQ

Is submitting a request the same as confirmation?

No. A request is Pending first. The team confirms the final date/time after review.

Can I choose my preferred date and time?

Yes. You can provide them; the team can confirm or reschedule.

Can I request online?

Yes. Select Online as the preferred mode. Final availability is confirmed by the team.

Can I use the AI chatbot?

Yes. You can start an appointment request directly through the StudentHubHelp AI Assistant.

What should I include?

Give a reachable phone number, purpose, preferred date/time and useful context. Avoid passwords, OTPs and payment information.
